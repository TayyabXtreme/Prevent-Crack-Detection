# Demo image credits

`crack_*.jpg` and `no_crack_*.jpg` are samples from the Kaggle dataset
[oluwaseunad/concrete-and-pavement-crack-images](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/oluwaseunad/concrete-and-pavement-crack-images),
the same dataset used to train the model (so the model may have seen some of them during training).

`crack_07.webp`, `crack_08.tiff` (grayscale) and `no_crack_07.png` (semi-transparent) are converted copies,
included to demonstrate that the app accepts any image format.
